OUR PAYMENT PROCESS

How to Request and Pay for a Service

  • Send an enquiry describing the service you need.
  • We review the request and confirm the service scope and price.
  • You receive payment instructions before paid work begins.
  • If requested, submit proof of payment through the agreed channel.
  • Our office verifies the payment and confirms the next steps.

Security note: Sending proof of payment does not itself confirm payment or activate customer portal access. Access, where applicable, is enabled only after office verification.
