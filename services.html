A CLEAR PROCESS

How Our Service Process Works

We aim to make the next steps clear before you commit to a paid service.

  • Tell us which service you need.
  • We review your enquiry and explain the scope of assistance.
  • We confirm the price and payment instructions before paid work starts.
  • Where applicable, you can submit proof of payment for office verification.
  • We explain the next steps and provide updates as agreed.

Important: Final admission, funding, hiring and application decisions are made by the relevant institution, funder or employer. We do not guarantee acceptance, funding or employment.
